About

John is an experienced healthcare professional with over a decade of expertise in occupational rehabilitation, functional capacity evaluations, injury management, and medical treatment. His dual qualifications in Exercise Physiology and Chiropractic care allow him to assess and manage complex Workcover and SIRA claims with a holistic, evidence-based approach.

He works closely with injured workers, insurers, and legal representatives to facilitate timely, durable return-to-work outcomes — drawing on deep experience in functional assessments, return-to-work planning, and injury prevention. John is particularly skilled in managing complex and stagnated claims, with a proven track record of navigating legal and insurance frameworks to reach optimal case resolutions.

He has worked with a wide range of clients across ADF, DVA, healthcare, construction, and corporate sectors — building strong rapport with everyone in the claims process, from injured workers to insurers, employers, and legal teams.

Beyond one-on-one casework, John delivers Manual Handling training and injury prevention seminars for workplaces, conducts pre-employment physical assessments, and manages complex neurological and autoimmune presentations alongside standard rehabilitation. He is also a Registered NDIS Provider (Treatment), and has shared his approach to recovery on industry podcasts including AusRehab's Behind the Claims Podcast.
